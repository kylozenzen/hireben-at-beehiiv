  • Interview 15+ active beehiiv creators across tiers — find the words they use, not the words we use

  • Rewrite core positioning to fully reflect "operating system for the creator economy" — not just a newsletter tool

  • Make the 0% revenue share comparison impossible to ignore: a $200K creator leaving $20K on Substack's table is a headline, not a footnote
  • Launch one major feature campaign using the new framework as a live test

  • A/B test homepage positioning against trial-to-paid conversion — every point of lift is real ARR
  • Build a creator spotlight engine that generates social proof at scale and feeds both organic and paid
  • Establish a PMM ↔ Sales feedback loop: win/loss data updates messaging within a week, not a quarter
  • Present full positioning audit and Q3 roadmap to leadership with concrete metric targets attached
